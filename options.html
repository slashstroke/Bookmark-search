Bookmark Search

Off by default. When on, the hostname of each bookmarked site (for example github.com) is sent once to DuckDuckGo's icon service to download its icon, which is then saved on this device. Nothing else is sent, and local or private addresses are never sent. You can also withdraw this in about:addons under Permissions and data.

Open the popup with Ctrl+Shift+F, or type b and a space in the address bar. You can change the shortcut in about:addons (gear icon, then Manage Extension Shortcuts).
